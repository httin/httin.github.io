## I am

I'm **Tin Huynh**, based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Mathematics took me through middle school and high school. Engineering came later, and it uses a different test for being right. In mathematics, a proof that works in a billion cases and fails in one is dead. In engineering, getting more than half of it right is already valuable. I stumbled over that difference for a long time before I got used to it.

## I work

At university I built circuits and robots, and I worked with sensors and microcontrollers. In my final year, this robot took all the time and money I had.

## I write

I love it when math and programming meet. Writing those ideas down and sharing them is what sharpens my mind and nurtures my passion. I find happiness not only in the final results but also in the process of striving.

Most of what I publish is data structures, algorithms, and mathematics. Those posts live on the [Blogs](/blogs/) page.
